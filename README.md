🛠️ Maker GPS: Interactive Lean Canvas
A streamlined, interactive business planning tool specifically designed for makers, artists, and small-batch manufacturers. This project was developed for Innovation Week at the Centre for Research and Innovation.

🚀 Overview
Most business plans are too long and never get used. The Maker GPS is a one-page "living" document that helps you navigate the transition from a great idea to a sustainable income.

This tool allows you to:

Map your strategy across the 9 essential Lean Canvas blocks.

Calculate viability instantly using the Cost × 3 Rule.

Export your plan as a clean, high-quality PDF for your records or mentor meetings.

📏 The Cost × 3 Rule
One of the biggest reasons maker businesses fail is underpricing labor and overhead. This tool includes a built-in calculator to ensure your retail price covers:

Materials: Every screw, gram of filament, or sheet of wood.

Labor: Your time is a real expense—pay yourself fairly.

Overhead & Fees: Shipping, platform fees (Etsy/Shopify), and tool maintenance.

The Formula: (Materials + Labor + Fees) × 3 = Minimum Retail Price

💻 Technical Stack
This is a lightweight, self-hosted utility built with:

Tailwind CSS for a clean, modern responsive UI.

html2pdf.js for client-side PDF generation.

FontAwesome for intuitive iconography.

📖 Instructions
Identify the Problem: Don't start with your product; start with the pain your customer feels.

Define Your Segment: Who is your "Early Adopter"? Be specific (e.g., "Homeowners in Toronto who love mid-century modern decor").

Test Your Assumptions: Use the "Talk to 5 Strangers" rule mentioned in the session.

Save & Iterate: Your canvas is never "finished." Update it as you learn more about your market.
